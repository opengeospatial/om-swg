# Observation and Sampling of a Smiley Face
## Observations, Measurements and Samples 101

OMS is very useful, but often not easy to understand, especially for newbies. 
In addition, when presentations are focused on a specific environmental media, e.g. air quality, all members of the audience not working on this medium stop listening.
With this insight, we crafted a simple presentation describing OMS on the use case of determining the color of a smiley face sticker.
Based on popular demand, this has been extended to cover all aspects of the OMS model (e.g. collections and sampling), formalized into an OGC Technical Paper
